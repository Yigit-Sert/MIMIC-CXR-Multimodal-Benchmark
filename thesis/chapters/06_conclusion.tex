\chapter{Conclusion and Future Work}
\label{ch:conclusion}

\section{Summary of Findings}
This thesis provides a comparative analysis of visual, textual, and multimodal modeling paradigms on the MIMIC-CXR dataset.

Key takeaways include:
\begin{enumerate}
    \item Text-only models yield high metrics primarily due to \textit{Data Leakage}, limiting their utility as independent diagnostic systems.
    \item Image-only models carry inherent risks of \textit{Shortcut Learning} by relying on external hardware artifacts.
    \item Multimodal Fusion and Vision-Language Pretraining (MedCLIP) maximize clinical reliability and interpretability.
\end{enumerate}

\section{Future Work}
Future directions involve integrating Medical Large Language Models (MLLMs like MedGemma) with Structural Causal Models (SCMs) to eliminate demographic bias and advance causal medical AI.
